# Legacy snapshot

This `legacy/` directory preserves the earlier exploratory development tree as
it stood before the offline statistical verification package was released. It
is kept for provenance and reproducibility of intermediate analyses only.

- The frozen, manuscript-aligned results and verification scripts live at the
  repository root (`README.md`, `scripts/`, `data/clean_main/`,
  `data/ablation_kb/`, `data/sensitivity_t80/`).
- Files under `legacy/` are historical code, cached outputs, and draft reports
  that may use different seeds, naming, and assumptions. They are **not** the
  results reported in the manuscript and must not be cited as such.
- No API keys or account credentials are included.
